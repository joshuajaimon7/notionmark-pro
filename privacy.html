Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

NotionMark Pro is developed by TinyStack Labs. We are committed to complete user privacy. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any user data, clipped articles, or browsing habits.

2. Data Collection and Local Processing

  • 100% Client-Side Conversion: All HTML-to-Markdown parsing, table conversions, and YAML frontmatter generation execute entirely on your device inside Google Chrome.
  • No Server Uploads: Webpage text, research notes, and downloaded .md files are never transmitted to external servers.
  • Zero Tracking: The extension contains no analytics, telemetry, or user trackers.

3. Permissions

  • activeTab & scripting: Used strictly to read the active document when you click to clip an article.
  • storage: Used strictly on your local device to store frontmatter preferences and license key.
